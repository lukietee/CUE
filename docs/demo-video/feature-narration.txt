This is CUE, a producer for solo livestreamers. It plans your show, listens to your chat, and puts the right moment on screen.

Start with one line: an IRL city walk where chat picks the next challenge. Choose a directing style, and CUE drafts the show.

You get an editable run of show. Title, category, tags, and timed segments, all yours to change before you go live.

Connect Twitch for chat and channel details. Connect OBS over its local WebSocket, and stage CUE's transparent overlay right in your scene.

Going live always takes your confirmation. CUE never starts a broadcast on its own.

Now chat starts talking. Viewers keep asking for a challenge. CUE's director spots the pattern, checks your permissions and cooldown, and decides to bring chat into the show.

The cue lands in OBS as a real overlay on your stream, and the overlay server confirms the browser source received it.

Every decision is explained in the director feed: what it observed, why it acted, and the chat messages behind it.

And you stay in control. Pause the director, switch its style, or set how often it's allowed to cue.

When the show ends, CUE saves a recap with highlights and tips for next time, ready to export.

CUE. Your stream, right on cue.
