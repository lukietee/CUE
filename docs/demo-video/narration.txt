Meet Cue, your AI livestream producer. Plan your show, connect your studio, and bring your audience into the action, all from one desktop workspace.

Start with a simple brief. We're planning a city walk where chat picks the next challenge. Choose a director style, then create your stream plan.

Cue turns the brief into an editable running order. Refine your title, category, tags, and timed segments before the show begins.

Connect Twitch and OBS, then stage the Cue overlay in your scene. This walkthrough uses Demo Mode to demonstrate the workflow without starting a public broadcast.

Run the readiness check, review the confirmation, and start the session. Cue keeps broadcast controls explicit, so you decide when the show goes live.

Now chat asks for a challenge. Cue spots the repeated topic and turns it into an audience prompt. The director feed shows the supporting messages, the decision, and the result.

You stay in control. Pause the director at any time to stop automatic cues, then resume when you're ready to bring chat back into the show.

When you're done, end the session and export a recap of your production actions and highlight suggestions. Cue. Your stream, right on cue.
